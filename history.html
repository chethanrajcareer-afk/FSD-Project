History

The Indian Air Force (IAF) was established on 8 October 1932. It started with a small fleet of aircraft and has grown into one of the world's most capable air forces.

Major Milestones

  • 1932 - Indian Air Force established.
  • 1947 - Participated in operations after Independence.
  • 1965 - Played a major role during the Indo-Pak War.
  • 1971 - Contributed significantly to India's victory.
  • 1999 - Conducted Operation Safed Sagar during the Kargil War.

Today, the IAF operates modern fighter aircraft, helicopters, transport aircraft, and advanced defense systems.
